Anyuan Hakka

Speaking Anyuan Hakka together, documenting Anyuan together

Anyuan Hakka is the Hakka spoken in Anyuan County, Ganzhou City, Jiangxi Province, China. Each town and village has its own ways of speaking, and with them, its own knowledge of everyday life.

AYHAK is a community language documentation project started by people from Anyuan. Together we record the words, sounds, stories, and everyday knowledge of Anyuan Hakka, and make room for anyone who wants to learn it or keep speaking it.

Questions

Is Anyuan Hakka a language or a dialect?
In China it is usually called a dialect (方言). It is a variety of Hakka with its own sounds, words, and grammar.
I'm from Anyuan. How can I participat?
Come to a meetup, share words or stories from your town or village, or help with recordings. Any level of fluency is welcome.
I don't speak Anyuan Hakka. Can I join?
Yes. The upcoming lessons are taught in Mandarin, and listeners are welcome at the meetups.
How is Anyuan Hakka written?
The dictionary uses Chinese characters and the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA). Where a word has no agreed written form, we say so.
How will my recordings be used?
Only with your consent, and credited the way you choose. You can ask us to change or withdraw them at any time.

About

AYHAK is a community project started by Anyuan native speakers. We work by a few simple principles:

  • Speakers take part as collaborators.
  • Every town and village's way of speaking counts. There is no single "correct" Anyuan Hakka.
  • What we make goes back to the community first.
